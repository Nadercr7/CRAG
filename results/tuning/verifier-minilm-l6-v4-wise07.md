# Verifier tuning on HotpotQA dev: verifier-minilm-l6-v4-wise07

Generated by `scripts/tune_dev.py`. 200 dev questions, retrieval top-20 (hybrid), slices supportive and missing. Only dev questions are used. Thresholds are applied to Platt-calibrated probabilities fitted on the same candidates (in-sample; 5-fold question-grouped CV in the last column of the first table).

'Before' is sigmoid(raw logit). 10 equal-width bins. Fitted maps: passage (all retrieved) p = sigmoid(0.734 * logit + -2.379); sentence (selected chunks, used) p = sigmoid(0.645 * logit + -2.524); sentence (all retrieved chunks) p = sigmoid(0.757 * logit + -2.900).

The sentence calibrator is fitted on the sentences of the chunks the pipeline selects (top_k_context with prob >= tau_chunk), because refinement only ever scores those; the base rate of supporting facts there is much higher than among all 20 retrieved chunks, and the all-retrieved fit (shown for comparison, not saved) would put every sentence far too low for the keep threshold.

## Sentence keep threshold (supportive slice)

Over the chunks selected under tau_chunk 0.01 (top 8); the sentence calibrator is fitted on exactly those chunks. Recall = supporting facts kept / supporting facts inside those chunks. 'Capped' applies max_sentences = 8 as the pipeline does; the rule uses the capped columns. 'All facts kept' needs every supporting fact of the question (also those outside the selected chunks); 'all selected facts kept' only those inside.

Where the supporting facts are before refinement (all supportive questions): 97.7 % in the retrieved top-20, 95.8 % in the top 8 by verifier logit, 94.7 % in the selected chunks; 89.0 % of questions have all their facts in the selected chunks (the ceiling of 'all facts kept').
